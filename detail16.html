Living Archive

Design and visual development of an experiential project reimagining Singapore’s herbarium through glass-inspired botanical storytelling.

Living Archive transforms preserved plant specimens into contemporary visual forms, reimagining Singapore’s herbarium as a living narrative rather than a static archive.

By bridging science and art, the project inspires ecological awareness, curiosity, and a shared responsibility toward biodiversity.
